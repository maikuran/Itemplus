# Lunar / Martian gateways and lunar equipment

## Entering the dimensions

1. Craft `thesolar:moon_gateway` with obsidian, moon crystals, star dust and a diamond.
2. Place the Moon Gateway and walk into its glowing face. It transfers the player to `thesolar:moon`.
3. Craft `thesolar:mars_gateway` with obsidian, blood crystals, a Martian sigil and a diamond.
4. Place the Mars Gateway and walk into it to enter `thesolar:mars`.
5. Each gateway is bidirectional: walking into the same gateway from its destination dimension returns the player to the Overworld.

Gateways are placeable blocks, not frame-activated vanilla Nether portals. They are available in the Building Blocks creative tab and have crafting recipes.

## Lunar ore equipment

- Selenite: complete tool set and complete armor set. Two armor pieces grant Slow Falling; four grant Night Vision and Slow Falling.
- Lunarium: complete tool set and complete armor set. Two armor pieces grant Resistance; four grant Resistance and Strength.
- Wearing either lunar boots set on the Moon grants Speed.
- Selenite and Lunarium have separate ore drops, smelting/blasting recipes, repair tags and armor equipment textures.

## Combat balance

Lunar and Martian hostile mobs have increased health and attack damage. Selene has 1,600 health; Ares has 2,200 health. Their existing wind-up attacks and second phases remain active, with a stronger second-phase multiplier.
